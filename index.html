Real Madrid tactical analysis on YouTube

Madrid Analytics is an independent YouTube channel that publishes original tactical analysis of Real Madrid matches: pre-match previews and post-match reviews covering formations, key moments, goals, VAR decisions and controversies, explained on a tactics board.

Madrid Analytics Uploader

Madrid Analytics Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos (video file, title, description, thumbnail and playlist) to the Madrid Analytics YouTube channel. It is not available to the public and has no user accounts.
